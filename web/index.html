To get a PDF or a printout at the right size:
  1. Use a Chromium-based browser if you can (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Chromium…).
  2. Destination: "Save as PDF" (or your printer).
  3. Scale: 100% (actual size), never "Fit to page".
  4. Margins: "None" or "Default" (the page already handles its margins).
Print a test page and check that a slip has the size you set.
